Available models

Chats are saved locally, with a previous-version backup. They are not automatically synced between devices. Your prompts and any permitted tool results are sent directly to Corvex.

Computer and folder access reset when you close Hearth. The app cannot send emails, run arbitrary commands or browse websites. Drafts and normal app-close requests require approval.

Dictation uses macOS facilities where available. Enable Dictation in System Settings → Keyboard and use your Mac’s dictation shortcut. Depending on your Mac and settings, speech may be processed by Apple. No speech model is bundled.
