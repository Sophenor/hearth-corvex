HEARTH FOR MAC - PREVIEW 0.1

Requirements: macOS 13 Ventura or newer, on an Apple Silicon or Intel Mac,
an internet connection, and your own Corvex Token Factory account/API key.
You do not need a GitHub account, OpenAI subscription or local AI model.

1. GET YOUR CORVEX ACCOUNT READY
   Start at https://www.corvex.ai/token-factory and complete your signup.
   Credit amounts and expiry depend on Corvex and your invitation.
   Hearth itself does not grant free credits.

2. DOWNLOAD THE MAC APP
   https://github.com/Sophenor/hearth-corvex/releases/tag/mac-v0.1.0
   Under Assets, choose Hearth-Mac.zip, NOT the source-code ZIP.

3. INSTALL
   Open your Downloads folder and double-click Hearth-Mac.zip.
   Drag the extracted Hearth app into Applications. Open it there.
   If macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet,
   check that it came from the GitHub link above and choose Open.

4. IF APPLE BLOCKS THE FIRST OPEN
   This independent preview has no Apple Developer ID signature or
   notarization. macOS may say it cannot verify the developer.
   After trying to open it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   Find the notice about Hearth, choose Open Anyway, and confirm Open.
   Your Mac may ask you to authenticate. This is a one-time exception
   for this app, not an instruction to turn off Mac security.
   If your Mac instead reports malware or a damaged app, stop and ask
   for help. Do not disable Gatekeeper, antivirus or other protections.
   An employer/university-managed Mac may prevent this exception.

5. CONNECT ONCE
   In Hearth, click the link to your Corvex API keys page, or visit:
   https://tokenfactory.corvex.cloud/app/settings/keys
   Sign in to Corvex in your browser and create an API key.
   Copy the key, return to Hearth, paste it, and click Connect.
   Use the API key, NOT your Corvex password. Treat it like a password:
   never send it to other people or put it in a group chat.
   Hearth stores it in your Mac's Keychain, not in exported chats.

6. START CHATTING
   Try: "Help me write a friendly email about..."
   Chats save automatically on this Mac. Use New chat for a new topic,
   click an old chat in the sidebar to continue, or use Export to save
   a readable copy. Choose a model from your live Corvex catalogue.
   Changing models does not require another app download, although a
   new model's tool compatibility is not guaranteed.

7. OPTIONAL COMPUTER AND FILE HELP
   Turn on Computer help if you want a snapshot of Mac performance,
   free disk space and visible running apps. Ask, "Why might my Mac
   feel slow?" Hearth asks you separately before requesting a normal
   app quit. Save your work before approving a quit.
   Choose one folder to let Hearth list it and read small UTF-8 text
   files. Files it reads may be sent to Corvex. Avoid sensitive folders.
   Saving a .txt, .md or .csv draft requires approval of its exact path
   and text; an existing version is backed up. It cannot delete files,
   force-quit apps, run arbitrary commands or control your whole Mac.
   These permissions reset when you close Hearth.

8. OPTIONAL DICTATION
   Enable Dictation in System Settings > Keyboard, click the message
   box, and use the shortcut shown in those settings. The Dictate
   button also attempts to start the system's dictation feature.
   This uses macOS, with no large speech model bundled into Hearth.
   Availability, privacy and accuracy depend on your Mac/settings;
   Apple may process audio. Check the words before clicking Send.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
   No connection: check your internet, key, account balance and model.
   Rate limit: wait before trying again. Automatic retry loops are not
   used. A stopped request may still incur Corvex charges.
   Keychain prompt: the app needs permission to store/read its own key.
   A future preview build may prompt again. Never put your Mac login
   password into the chat or Corvex key field.
   Replies may take a while; this preview displays the final answer
   rather than streaming individual words. Stop cancels the request.
   Normal conversations are local to this Mac, not synced to Windows
   or Corvex's web chat. Data lives at:
   ~/Library/Application Support/HearthCorvex
   The app's Open data folder control opens this location.
   Disconnect removes Hearth's saved API key; saved chats remain.

LIMITS
   This preview does not browse the live web, read PDF/Word/images,
   send emails, provide full desktop automation or guarantee correct
   AI answers. Its model runs at Corvex, not locally on your Mac.
   Selected messages and permitted tool results go directly to Corvex
   and use your own balance. There is no shared family API key.

The Windows download and Windows instructions remain separate:
